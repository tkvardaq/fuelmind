Change your PIN

Change it whenever you think someone else has seen it — you do not need to ask anyone. You will be signed out everywhere once it changes, so sign in again with the new PIN.

If you forget it

On the sign-in page, choose your name and use I've forgotten my PIN. That tells the station owner, who can set you a new one. It does not let anyone in by itself.

{{if .Me.IsOwner}}

You are an owner. If you are the only owner and you forget your PIN, nobody can reset it from the dashboard. On the shop PC itself, run fuelmind-setup reset-pin as an administrator — physical access to the machine is the last way back in, and it is meant to be.
