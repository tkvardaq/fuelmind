Ask from your phone

{{if .CloudOn}}

With this on, you can ask about this station by WhatsApp or text message when you are away, and the answer comes from this PC. The station is never opened to the internet: it asks the FuelMind service whether you have sent a question, answers it here, and sends the answer back. The answer itself (for example today's revenue) passes through that service, so leave this off if you would rather it never left the shop.
